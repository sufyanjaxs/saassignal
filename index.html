Why we exist

Vendor sites rank for vendor keywords. Review sites rank for "best X" because someone tried to sell you something. StackSignal is built so the honest comparison wins the click — which is why we publish the scoring criteria before the verdict, not after.

How we make money

Display ads and affiliate commissions. Neither is allowed to buy a rating. If a tool has a weak category, the review says so. Our full policy is public, including how to advertise without buying a review.

What we don't do

No pay-to-rank placements. No invented "10 best" lists with tools we never opened. No courses. No autoresponders pretending to be a human. If we cannot reproduce the number, it does not ship.
