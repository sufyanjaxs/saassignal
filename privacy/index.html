Privacy Policy

Who runs this site

StackSignal is operated by Sufyan Khan. Contact details are on the contact page.

What we collect

Three things, and only three:

  • Newsletter email. If you subscribe, we store your email address with our email provider in order to send the monthly email. Every issue carries a one-click unsubscribe.
  • Aggregate analytics. Page views, referrer, and country, collected through a privacy-respecting analytics provider. We do not build profiles of individual visitors and we do not sell or share visitor data with advertisers.
  • Comments. We currently do not run comments. If that changes, the form will say what is stored before you post.

Cookies

Advertising partners — including Google — may set cookies or use similar technologies to serve and measure ads. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out at Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.

Affiliate links

Some links to vendors are affiliate links. We may earn a commission when you buy through them. Commissions never influence which tools we recommend or how we score them. See the full disclosure.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you may ask us to show the personal data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email us and we will action it within 30 days. We do not sell personal information, full stop.

Children

This site is not directed at anyone under 16 and we knowingly collect no data from children.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected in the "last updated" date below. Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept the revised policy.
