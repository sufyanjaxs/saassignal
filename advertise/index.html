Advertise

We accept a small number of clearly-labelled placements. The rules below are the whole policy — there is no tier system and no "premium package" that buys editorial treatment.

What you can buy

  • Display advertising. Responsive display units in a marked ad slot. Sold on predicted pageviews and engagement for the placement, never per-article.
  • A sponsored guide. We choose the topic, research it, and write it. You supply product access and a factual briefing. We publish the conclusion we actually reached, and it may be negative.
  • A newsletter mention. Clearly marked, described in one line, links labelled.
  • A vendor listing. A neutral entry in the relevant roundup or tools page, at the same rate for every vendor, placed by category rather than by payment tier.

What you cannot buy

  • Placement in a ranking or "best of" table.
  • A score or rating, or the removal of one.
  • Word count, headline, or the conclusion.
  • Removal of a negative section.
  • A "no ads" promise on an editorial page.
  • Any claim that a review was independent if it was not.

Why we are strict

The entire value of this site is that a reader believes the comparison. Selling conclusions destroys that in one transaction and it does not come back. Several sites larger than ours make that trade; we would rather be small and believed.

How to enquire

Use the contact form with the subject "Advertising". Tell us the product, the target audience, and the placements you want. We reply within three business days with our rate card. We do not supply traffic figures we cannot verify.
